## Your function

def group_anagrams(strs: list[str]) -> list[list[str]]

**Example 2.** `group_anagrams(strs = ["abc", "def", "ghi", "jkl"])` returns `[["abc"], ["def"], ["ghi"], ["jkl"]]`.
